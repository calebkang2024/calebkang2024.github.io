01 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Curious by nature.
Creative by instinct.

I'm drawn to games because they bring together two things I have always loved: playing and storytelling.

I love reading books, but I see games as one of the most immersive ways to tell a story. Through visuals, music, environments, character interactions, and player choices, games can communicate in ways that go beyond the written page and let people experience a story for themselves.

My interest in building games also grew from discovering that I understood computer logic and enjoyed programming. I started experimenting with Scratch, creating my own games and becoming interested in how interactive experiences are designed and built.

I'm now studying Computer Science at Drexel University and exploring many sides of game development: gameplay programming and mechanics, narrative design, progression systems, and building games both independently and with a team. I'm especially interested in audio design, where my experience with music and sound can become part of the player experience.

Music has been a major part of my life for years. I've played cello since fourth grade and have also participated in orchestra. Outside of that, I've composed music for theater, worked as the sound head for a production, and gained practical audio experience through a podcast audio engineering co-op. Those experiences have made me interested in how sound, music, and interactive design work together. I'm less drawn to creating visual art and assets, but I enjoy the many other creative and technical disciplines that shape a game.

Jujutsu Kaisen: Shibuya Survivors

Jujutsu Kaisen: Shibuya Survivors is an in-development, top-down bullet heaven game inspired by Vampire Survivors. Players control a character, use combat abilities, fight waves of cursed spirits, and take on boss encounters.

My contributions include designing the player, movement, abilities, attacks, stats, UI, and progression systems. I use AI-assisted development with OpenAI Codex to help implement my ideas; the core concepts and design decisions are my own. The current playable build includes character combat, abilities, enemy encounters, and a boss fight, while most broader systems are still in development.
